About the Author

Prasanth N.M. writes gripping novels across horror, psychological thriller, romance, mystery, suspense, dark fantasy, and supernatural fiction. His books focus on powerful atmosphere, emotional tension, secrets, betrayal, haunted places, and memorable twists.

With cinematic storytelling and a classic love for strong plots and page-turning suspense, Prasanth N.M. creates stories for readers who enjoy mystery, emotion, fear, and unforgettable endings.
