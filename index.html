About the Author

Prasanth N.M. writes gripping novels across horror, psychological thriller, romance, mystery, suspense, and dark fiction. His stories explore fear, love, secrets, betrayal, haunted places, emotional darkness, and the hidden side of human nature.

With cinematic storytelling and a strong traditional love for powerful plots, memorable characters, and page-turning suspense, Prasanth N.M. creates books for readers who enjoy atmosphere, emotion, and twists.
